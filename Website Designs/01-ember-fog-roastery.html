Every bag traces a curve.

This is the actual profile for our Guji Natural — 9 minutes, 42 seconds, drum temperature tracked from charge to drop.

01

Charge

Green beans drop into a 400°F drum; temperature dips as they absorb heat.

02

Drying

Moisture evaporates. Beans turn from grassy green to pale yellow.

03

First Crack

Sugars caramelize audibly. This is where flavor development truly begins.

04

Drop

Beans hit the cooling tray the instant they reach our target curve.

From cherry to cup, we track it all.

i.

Cupping & selection

Every incoming lot is cupped blind against three references before it's approved.

ii.

Profile roasting

Each origin gets its own logged curve, tuned in a test roast before the full batch.

iii.

Rest & degas

Beans rest a minimum of 5 days, releasing CO₂ before they're sealed.

iv.

Bag & ship

Packed within 48 hours of your order, never off a warehouse shelf.
