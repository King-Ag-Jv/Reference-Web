Studios — Bergen & Kyoto

Three things every project shares, regardless of scale.

Material honesty

Timber reads as timber, concrete as concrete. We rarely finish over what a material can say on its own.

Long daylight studies

Every scheme is modeled through a full year of sun before it's approved for construction.

Joinery over adhesive

Where we can detail a joint instead of a seam, we do — buildings should be repairable, not disposable.
